Introduction

Every exercise is a step.
Do them honestly. Without censorship. Without self-deception.

This workbook is not just a notebook. It's your personal transformation tool. You will write about what hurts. Capture small wins. See real progress.

In a month, you'll open it and won't recognize yourself.

There are no right answers here. Only yours. Write by hand, type, draw. The main thing is don't stop.

This workbook will witness your transformation. From victim to creator. From ashes to fiery wings.
